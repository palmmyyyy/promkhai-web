Android app

prom-khai

A point-of-sale app for small retail stores.

What it does

prom-khai is a point-of-sale (POS) app built for small retail shops. It runs entirely on the cashier's Android device — there is no server or cloud backend behind it. Product, stock, and sales data is stored locally on the device, so the app keeps working even without an internet connection.

Sign-in with Google

prom-khai offers an optional "Sign in with Google" option so a cashier can identify themselves inside the app. Signing in only reads your basic Google profile (name, email address, and profile picture) to display who is currently using the app. See our Privacy Policy for details on exactly what is accessed and how it's used.

Key features

  • Works fully offline — local SQLite storage on the device
  • Product catalog and stock tracking
  • No account or backend server required to use the app
  • Optional Google Sign-In for cashier identification
