Android app

PromKhai

PromKhai is a point-of-sale (POS) app for small retail stores, used by shop owners and cashiers to record sales and manage stock on an Android phone or tablet.

What PromKhai does

PromKhai lets a retail shop ring up sales, track product stock, and manage its product catalog directly from an Android device. The app runs entirely on that device — there is no server or cloud backend behind it. Product, stock, and sales data is stored locally on the device, so the app keeps working even without an internet connection.

Sign-in with Google

PromKhai offers an optional "Sign in with Google" option so a cashier can identify themselves inside the app. Signing in only reads your basic Google profile (name, email address, and profile picture) to display who is currently using the app. See our Privacy Policy for details on exactly what is accessed and how it's used.

Key features

  • Works fully offline — local SQLite storage on the device
  • Product catalog and stock tracking
  • No account or backend server required to use the app
  • Optional Google Sign-In for cashier identification
